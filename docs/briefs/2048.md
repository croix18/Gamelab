# 2048: brief

Slide the tiles; two matching tiles merge into one worth double. Math: powers of two, exponents, planning.

## Rules

- A 4×4 board. Slide up, down, left or right, and every tile moves as far as it can.
- Two equal tiles that meet merge into their sum. A tile merges once per slide.
- After every slide that moves something, a new tile appears: a 2 nine times in ten, otherwise a 4.
- Classic goal: make 2048, then keep going if you like. The game ends when no slide moves anything.

## Numbers or powers

Setup switches the tiles between numbers (32) and powers (2⁵), for every mode. Under the board, a line shows the slide's biggest merge as an equation: 32 + 32 = 64, or in powers 2⁵ + 2⁵ = 2⁶, so the exponent rule is in front of students on every slide.

## Modes

- **Play:**
  - **Classic.** The game saves in the browser, so it can be finished later. New game starts over.
  - **Challenge.** Reach a target tile within a slide limit. The starting limits are Easy 128 in 120 slides, Medium 256 in 220 and Hard 512 in 420, then they are set by simulation so a careful player makes them.
- **Daily:** the same tiles for everyone and 150 slides. The results card shows the final board as small colored squares, with the score, the biggest tile, and what the look-ahead player scored on the same tiles. A replay is marked as one.
- **Board:** for the panel. The class votes each slide and the teacher makes it. Classic or Challenge.

## Controls

Swipe on touch screens, or use the arrow keys or WASD. H for a hint, N for a new game. There's no undo.

## Hint

The look-ahead player suggests a slide and says why in a few words: "keeps your biggest tile in its corner", "opens space", "sets up a merge". Using a hint marks the round "with hints". There's no Show me, because 2048 has no single answer.

## Debrief, after every game

The score, the biggest tile, and the two or three slides that cost the most. Each is shown as a small board with the slide made beside the slide the look-ahead player preferred.

## Engine

The board is 16 exponents. Moves, merges and scoring follow the classic rules. The Daily and Challenge tiles come from a seeded stream, so the same slides give the same tiles. The look-ahead player is expectimax two or three slides deep, judging empty space, a corner anchor, ordered rows and possible merges, and answers in under 150 ms on a Chromebook.

## Look

Small tiles in sand and pale turquoise, deepening through turquoise and teal to navy. 2048 is gold. No coral. Mint accent (`#5FD3A4`, pale `#DDF6EC`) on the Game Lab kit.
